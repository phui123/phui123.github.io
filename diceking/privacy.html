骰皇 Dice King — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

骰皇 Dice King ("the app") does not collect personal information itself. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service described below collects.

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no analytics of our own.
  • Your virtual coin balance is saved only on your device, and is deleted when you delete the app.
  • If you buy "骰皇 Pro", the purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • When you open the app, and when you choose to watch an ad for more free rounds or coins, the app may show a full-screen ad from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device such as its advertising identifier (only if you allow it), IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with the ad. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps, so ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before any ads are shown. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The app is for entertainment only. Virtual coins have no real-world value and cannot be purchased or cashed out.
