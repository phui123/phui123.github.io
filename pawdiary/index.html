Paw Diary – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Paw Diary ("the app") does not collect any personal information.

  • Your diary stays on your device. Pet profiles, diary entries, notes, reminders, care instructions and photos are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The app never sends them anywhere.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising. The app contains no tracking or advertising software and does not connect to any server of ours.
  • Photos and camera. Photos you add are copied into the app. Camera access is requested only when you tap "Take a photo". You can use the app without it.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can use the app without allowing notifications.
  • Sharing and export. Information leaves the app only when you tap Share or Export and choose where to send it.
  • Backups. Like most apps, the app's data is included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup or computer backups if you use them, under your own Apple Account.
  • Purchases. The 14-day free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Deleting your data. Pet Profile › Data & privacy › "Delete all data" removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app does the same.

Paw Diary is a record-keeping tool. It does not give veterinary advice and does not calculate doses. Follow your veterinarian's instructions, and contact them with any question about your pet's health or medication. Reminders depend on your phone's notification settings and should not be your only safeguard for important medication.

Support

Questions or problems? Email phui123@gmail.com.

  • How does the free trial work? Every feature is free for 14 days. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks everything for good. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.
  • What happens if I do not unlock? Your diary stays readable and exportable, and reminders you already set up keep working, including recording a dose. Adding new entries, reminders and pets is paused.
  • I unlocked on another iPhone. Pet Profile › Data & privacy › Trial and unlock › Restore purchase.
  • I am not getting reminders. Open the Reminders tab; it shows whether notifications are allowed and how many are queued.
