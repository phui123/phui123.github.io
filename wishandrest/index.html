Wish & Rest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Wish & Rest ("the app") does not collect any personal information.

  • Your words and your voice stay on your iPhone. The routines, intentions and affirmations you write, and the voice recordings you make, are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. The app never sends them anywhere.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising. The app contains no tracking or advertising software and does not connect to any server of ours.
  • Microphone. Used only while you tap Record, to save an affirmation in your own voice. You can use the app without it.
  • Notifications. If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled on your device. Reminders never contain the text you wrote.
  • Spoken phrases. Phrases you have not recorded are read aloud by your iPhone's built-in voice, on the device.
  • Backups. Like most apps, the app's data is included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup or computer backups if you use them, under your own Apple Account. A switch in the app's Settings ("Keep out of backups") leaves routines and recordings out of backups.
  • Purchases. The optional Lifetime purchase and tips are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Deleting your data. Settings › "Delete all routines and recordings" removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app does the same.

Wish & Rest is for relaxation and reflection. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose or treat any condition, including sleep problems.

Support

Questions or problems? Email phui123@gmail.com.

  • I hear no voice. Open the routine, add an affirmation and tap Record, or leave "Read unrecorded phrases aloud" on so your iPhone reads it.
  • The screen went black. That is the dark screen for sleeping; tap anywhere to bring the player back. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • I bought Lifetime on another iPhone. Settings › Restore purchases.
