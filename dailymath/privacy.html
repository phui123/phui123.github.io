Daily Math Challenge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Daily Math Challenge ("the app") does not collect, store or share any personal information. It works offline.

  • No accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your answers, scores and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app keeps the date you first used it in the device's Keychain so the 7-day free trial can't be restarted by reinstalling. This date stays on the device.
  • Purchases of Daily Math Pro are handled by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

The app is suitable for children and collects no information from them.
