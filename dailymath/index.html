Daily Math Challenge

Ten math questions a day for grades 6 to 8, on iPhone and iPad. Topics you get wrong come back more often, and the results log shows your progress by grade.

Choosing the grade mix

Open Settings and set how much of each day's challenge comes from each grade level. The new mix applies from the next challenge.

Free trial and Pro

The daily challenge is free for the first 7 days. Daily Math Pro (monthly, every 3 months, or a one-time lifetime purchase) keeps the daily challenge going every day and adds unlimited extra rounds. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple ID settings (Settings › your name › Subscriptions). On a new device, open Settings › Restore purchases.

My results are gone

Results are stored on the device only. Deleting the app removes them; they are not backed up to a server.
